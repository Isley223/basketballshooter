HOW TO PLAY

Player Movement & Defender

Move around using A / D or Left / Right arrow keys.

Toggle the Defender AI on/off in the header. When enabled, the defender will jump to contest and attempt to block your shots!

Aiming & Shooting Controls

Method 1 (Touch / Mouse Drag): Click or touch ball, drag backwards to set arc angle & power preview line, and release to shoot!

Method 2 (Spacebar): Hold SPACE to charge shot power, release in green zone for clean arc!

Point Values & Crowd

  • Inside Paint: 1 Point | Mid-Range / FT: 2 Points
  • 3-Point Line: 3 Points | Logo Shot (Deep): 4 Points
  • Swish Bonus: +1 Point | Streak Multiplier: Up to 3x!
  • The indoor gym crowd on bleachers reacts to swishes, scores, and rejections!
