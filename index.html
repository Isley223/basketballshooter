HOW TO PLAY

Player Movement & Defender

Move with A / D or arrow keys. Press SHIFT or tap JUKE for a quick cut that can leave a nearby defender stumbling.

Toggle the Defender AI on/off in the header. When enabled, the defender will jump to contest and attempt to block your shots!

After an out-of-bounds turnover, check the ball at the three-point line. Guard the defender with the arrows and press SPACE to jump and block. If they miss, you get the ball at the three to check it up and attack.

Aiming & Shooting Controls

Method 1 (Touch / Mouse Drag): Click or touch ball, drag backwards to set arc angle & power preview line, and release to shoot!

Method 2 (Spacebar): When you have the ball, hold SPACE to charge and release to shoot. When defending, tap SPACE to jump and contest instead.
